## Conclusions for R4.3+

- Chipset/8-bit screens: ReadPixelArray8 + temp RastPort works.
- RTG truecolour: use cgx ReadPixelArray (LVO -120, a0 dest, d0 dx, d1 dy, d2 mod, a1 rp, d3 sx, d4 sy,
  d5 w, d6 h, d7 fmt) with RECTFMT_ARGB, then quantise or write a deep ILBM.
- Clipboard takes a 300 KB FORM ILBM.
