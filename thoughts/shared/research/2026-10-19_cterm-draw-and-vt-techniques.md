# CTerm vs vtcon: drawing and escapes

Source: ~/Code/creep-cterm (read only; MIT, keep the notice if code is ported). Read by grep and ranges of src/draw.c, src/vt.c, src/ppaint.s. Estimates are marked EST; none measured. Overlaps with the CCON doc (2026-10-19_ccon-latest-techniques.md) named in the last column of table 1. Our cost baseline (2026-10-19_vtcon-feed-perf.md): per 4268-byte frame on 020, vt_feed 49 ms, painter 61 ms, packets 12 ms; painter 106 us/cell against ~36 us bus-bound.

Finding that frames the rest: CTerm's planar painter (src/ppaint.s) is a port of CCON's pfused. So the drawing items are mostly CCON items seen from a second, documented implementation, with a safety layer (clip-rect test) CCON's notes did not describe. On escapes vtcon is ahead of CTerm almost everywhere (full Unicode 16 width table, DECRQSS, XTGETTCAP, sixel, kitty keyboard, OSC 8); the one clear gap is kitty graphics.

Worth porting directly (MIT, keep the notice): ppaint.s `pp_row` / `pp_row1` / `pp_row1z` (the group transpose is the point), draw.c `pp_cells` clip-rect and edge logic (lines 1445-1535), `pp_make`/`pp_glyph` store design. All four hinge on the same decision: replace BFINS with whole-long writes. Measure first on the rig with a one-plane synthetic row (bus-bound figure ~36 us/cell) before committing.

Refuted/low value to avoid: CTerm itself documents ScrollRaster at 12 ms a plane (draw.c:3075) and repaints scrolled rows on the planar painter; with the faster painter that may beat our BltBitMap scroll, but needs our own number.

## Overlap with the CCON doc

Rows 1-5 of table 1 are CCON items already ranked there: per-flush setup (rank 2 there), model-direct painter (3), register path (4), single-plane path (1). CTerm adds three things the CCON doc lacked: (a) the full pre-shifted glyph block layout and a readable, commented 477-line asm to port from, (b) the clip-rect rule that makes whole-long writes safe in a window, (c) the shown-blank table. No escape item overlaps CCON; CCON has no kitty graphics.

## Unverified / limits

- Gains are guesses from the cycle counts in the feed-perf note; nothing was run.
- vtcon blank-row skip, painter use of `used`, ?8452 and XTSMGRAPHICS were checked by grep only; absence is not proof (grep proves a spelling).
- I did not read ppaint.s past its header and the pp_row1 banner, nor the sixel/pic internals beyond headings.
