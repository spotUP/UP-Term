# T3: AmigaOS 3.2

Owner 2026-10-03: "the replay runs amigaos 3.2 will that make our console device crash?",
"we need to make our console device compatible with 3.2 not block it", "if you have a 3.2
install you can test with thats fine". The two refusals (UPConsole CON ON since DD20, DEVICE
ON since 5301c97) go when their checks are green on a real 3.2 -- not before.

## The rig

`rig.py start --os32`: Kickstart 47.115 (~/Desktop/KICK_323.rom, 3.2.3) and the owner's
installed AmigaOS 3.2 tree (~/Downloads/AmigaOS-3.2-full (1), Workbench 47.2, dos.library
47.30) copied to build/rig/os32 as DH0:. Native 4-colour Workbench (no RTG driver in that
tree). First boot 2026-10-03: XCON: opens and a Shell runs in it.

- [x] T3.2 DP5 on the real 3.2 (2026-10-03): the console sends CSI code;length;cursor+1 U and
      the line at once for TAB 12, Shift+TAB 13, Up 2, Down 3 (mediumprobe FORCE on the 3.2.3
      ROM con-handler: CSI 12;4;3U "abcd" with the cursor two left); typed lines are buffered
      until Return. The Shell answers with ACTION_FORCE (2001): Arg2 the line it made, Arg3 its

      list it writes a newline, SetMode(1), polls WAIT_CHAR, writes the list, SetMode(2),
      the prompt (prefixed 0x0F) and forces the line back. Measured through XCON:'s packet log
      (SERIAL build) with the 3.2 Shell. Was: medium mode's bytes -- mediumprobe_rig.py: TAB, Shift+TAB,
      Up, Down, what the reader gets and what the Shell answers; vary the typed text until
      the CSI ... U fields are decoded. Writes research/2026-10-03_v47-medium-mode.md.
