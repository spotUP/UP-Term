## Constraints / decisions

- ONE vamos at a time; never FS-UAE. vamos's 68020 BFINS is wrong for fields spanning five
  bytes, so the painter's asm-vs-C check (engbench ONLY 9) is a RIG step; instruction counts
  under vamos are fine.
- Earlier measurements on the stock rig (d11df66, 6263839): aligned longs with
  read-modify-write at the edges and a 4x4 transpose were SLOWER than the BFINS byte loop.
  Instruction count is the lever on this machine; BFINS stays.
- WaitForChar stays honest: nothing here defers drawing past a barrier.
- creep's code: notes read for ideas only (no licence); no code copied.
